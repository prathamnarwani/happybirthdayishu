Happy birthday, my love.

I've tried to write this for days, and every draft came out too small for what I mean. So I'll keep it simple and true.

You make my life feel like it's happening on purpose. Not louder or busier, just more real and more warm. I notice how you go quiet when something matters, how you soften when you think nobody's watching, and how you make ordinary hours feel like they were worth waiting for.

I don't say it as often as I feel it, so I'm saying it here, where it can stay: I'm proud of you, I'm grateful for you, and I'm still amazed you chose me back.

This year, I hope you get everything you're quietly working toward. I hope you rest without guilt and laugh until it hurts, and I hope you always know someone is on your side, with no conditions and no scoreboard.

I'll be right here, happily caught.

Yours, always.

The hunt is over. You're caught, you're kept, and you're loved.
